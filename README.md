# Passport Photo Kit Support

Welcome to the support page for **Passport Photo Kit: ID & Visa**.

Passport Photo Kit makes passport, visa and ID photos on your iPhone. Choose a document, then take or choose a photo. The app crops and scales the photo to that document's published size rules and checks it. You can save a single photo or a print-ready sheet. Your photos stay on your iPhone.

If you have a question, find a problem or want a new document, you are in the right place.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my photo be accepted?**  
A: The app prepares the photo to the published size rules of the document you choose. Whether a photo is accepted is decided by the issuing authority.

**Q: Are my photos uploaded anywhere?**  
A: No. Photos are processed only on your device. The app has no server and never sends your photos anywhere. See the [Privacy Policy](PrivacyPolicy.md).

**Q: Does the app retouch my face?**  
A: No. It only crops, scales and slightly straightens the photo. It can also replace the background and correct the light. For documents whose rules discourage this, these are off by default and show a warning. Most authorities do not allow retouching of the face.

**Q: How do I print the photos?**  
A: On the Export screen, choose a sheet: 10 × 15 cm, 13 × 18 cm, L size or A4. Print it at 100 % (actual size), not "fit to page". If the sheet shows a 50 mm line, check it with a ruler.

**Q: What does Pro include? Is it a subscription?**  
A: Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks saving and sharing at full resolution without the watermark, and the print sheets, for every document and custom size. You can share it with your family through Family Sharing.

**Q: I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?**  
A: Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases".

**Q: My document is not in the list.**  
A: Use "Custom size" to enter the width and height, or ask for the document below.
