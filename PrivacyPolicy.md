# Privacy Policy — Passport Photo Kit

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Passport Photo Kit: ID & Visa ("the app") is made by Enes Nurullah Kendirci ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

### Your photos stay on your iPhone
- Photos you take or choose are processed **on your device only**. The app never uploads them, and we never receive them.
- The app does not keep your photos. A photo is held in memory while you prepare it. When you save or share it, the app writes the file you asked for. Saved photos go to your Photos library, shared photos go wherever you send them, and the temporary copy is in a folder the system clears.
- We do not use your photos to train anything, and we do not show them to anyone.

### Face measurements
- To check and crop the photo, the app measures the position of the face on your device: eyes, chin, head size and tilt. These measurements are used only to prepare that photo.
- They are not stored, sent, or used to recognise or identify anyone. The app does not create or keep biometric identifiers.

### Purchases
- The one-time Pro purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
- We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to confirm purchases and to see purchase statistics. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, and your device's locale and currency code. It never receives your photos or face measurements. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### No ads, no tracking
- The app shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no analytics about your photos.
- The app stores only your recent document choices and custom sizes on your device. You can clear them in Settings.

### Permissions
- **Camera:** to take the photo. Used only while the camera screen is open.
- **Add to Photos:** to save the finished photo. The app does not read your photo library. When you choose a photo, the system picker gives the app only that photo.

### Children
Parents can make photos of their children. The same on-device processing applies, and nothing is uploaded.

### Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: enesnkendirci@gmail.com.
